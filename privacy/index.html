Yearkick Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-05 · Applies to the Android app "Yearkick" (package com.yearkick.game)

1. Who we are

Yearkick ("the app", "we") is published by Tembris (Oğulcan Özkan), an individual developer.
Privacy contact: tembrisstudio@gmail.com.

For the limited data described below, we act as data controller under the GDPR and under Turkey's Law No. 6698 (KVKK). Google provides the advertising and purchase services described below and runs the Android and Google Play diagnostics described in section 4.3 (see section 8).

2. The short version

  • Yearkick is a single-player, offline football card and draft game for people aged 18 and over.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no game server and no cloud save. We do not run any server that receives your data.
  • Your career, cards, club and player names and settings are stored on your device.
  • The app itself sends no usage statistics and no crash reports. It contains no analytics or crash-reporting service.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads use Google's consent form (UMP) where the law requires it.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • If you have switched on "usage & diagnostics" sharing in your Android device, Google Play may receive crash and "app not responding" data about apps on your device, including Yearkick, and show us reports in Google Play Console (section 4.3). That is Google's collection, controlled by your device setting.
  • We do not sell your personal data. The data described here is pseudonymous, not anonymous (see section 6).

3. What stays on your device

Stored locally on your device, in the app's private storage, and not sent to us:

  • your save (career progress, cards, album, achievements, settings);
  • the club and player names you type;
  • technical diagnostics for fixing problems (screen/phase names, memory figures, the reason Android gave when the app last closed). These stay on the device unless you choose to share them with "Report a problem" (section 4.3).

This data leaves your device only if you export your save yourself (section 10), if Android backup is on (section 10), or if you choose to share a problem report (section 4.3).

4. Data that leaves your device, and when

4.1 Ads (Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform)

The app can show rewarded ads (only when you choose to watch one) and interstitial ads (at most one per season, at the end of a season, never in your first season, never in the Daily Draw).

  • AdMob receives and may use: your Android advertising ID and app set ID, IP address (from which it can estimate general location), device and app information, ad interactions (such as app launches, taps, video views, whether a reward was earned) and diagnostic information about app and SDK performance (such as launch time and hang rate), for advertising, ad measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Google describes this at developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure. This data is sent over encrypted connections (TLS).
  • Where the law requires it (for example the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland) the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. If the form is required and you do not consent, no ads are requested. Where available, you can reopen it from Settings. In other regions Google may serve ads without showing a separate prompt; see section 11 for your choices.
  • We configure ad requests as: not directed at children, no under-age-of-consent tag, maximum ad content rating "PG".
  • If your device region or time zone is set to Turkey, the app asks Google for non-personalised ads only: ads are not chosen on the basis of an advertising profile. Google may still use the data above for frequency capping, ad measurement and fraud prevention.
  • The app asks for ads/consent only after you finish the introduction, and keeps working normally without ads if ads cannot be loaded. If you own an item that removes ads (No Ads or Chairman Pack), ads are not shown.

4.2 Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. Google handles payment and its own records. The app receives, on your device, the purchase result, the product identifier and a purchase token so that it can unlock what you bought; the app does not send these to us or to any other server of ours, and we do not run a server that stores them. Refunds follow Google Play's rules.

4.3 Google Play and Android diagnostics (crashes and "app not responding")

Yearkick contains no crash-reporting code. Google Play and Android, however, can collect crash and "app not responding" (ANR) data from devices whose users have opted in to automatically share usage and diagnostics data. Google makes this available to developers in Google Play Console (Android vitals). We use it only to find and fix stability problems. This collection is done by Google, not by the app; it follows your device setting (usually under Settings, Google, Usage & diagnostics; the name and place vary by device and Android version), which you can change at any time. We do not receive your name, e-mail or Google account with it, and we cannot choose to collect this data for a particular person. Google's description.

Report a problem. Settings has a "Report a problem" row. Only when you tap it, the app prepares a plain-text report on your device: app and Android version, device brand and model, language, the names of the screens you visited last, recent app exit reasons and recent error messages. It contains no save content, club or player names, purchase data, advertising ID, e-mail or location. The report opens in your device's share sheet; you can read it and you decide whether and where to send it. Nothing is sent automatically. If you send it to us, we use it only to investigate and fix the problem and delete it once the problem is resolved, at the latest after 12 months.

4.4 Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules at most one local "Daily Draw" reminder per day (08:00 local time) on your device. There is no push server. The permission is requested after you complete your first season, and you can turn it off in Android settings.

5. What we do not collect

Through the app we do not collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, postal address, precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data, or payment card data. If you send us a problem report or write to us, we receive the address you send it from (section 4.3). The app sends no usage statistics, in-game events or crash reports. Save export/import uses Android's system share sheet and file picker and gives the app no broad file access.

6. "Pseudonymous", not "anonymous"

Advertising identifiers and the other data in section 4.1 are tied to your advertising ID, app set ID or IP address and to technical details such as device model and approximate location. They are personal data (pseudonymous), not anonymous data. The same applies to the device-level data Google collects in section 4.3.

8. Recipients and international transfers

  • Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited — AdMob, User Messaging Platform, Google Play (purchases; Android vitals diagnostics). Google may process data in the United States and other countries.
  • Advertisers and ad partners take part in ad delivery through Google's systems (see Google's ad technology provider information).
  • We have no other processors and we do not sell personal data.

For transfers outside the EEA/United Kingdom we rely on the safeguards offered by Google (such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework certification or standard contractual clauses). For users in Turkey, transfers abroad follow Art. 9 of KVKK; see the Turkish version of this policy.

9. Children

Yearkick is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child is using the app, contact us at tembrisstudio@gmail.com.

10. Backup and export

  • Android Auto Backup: your save (the app's mmkv storage) may be backed up to your own Google account and restored or transferred to a new device. This is handled by Android and Google, not by us.
  • Export/import: you can export your save or a share card through the system share sheet and import a save file later. The file is yours; it is not sent to us.

11. Your rights and how to use them

Because the app has no account and we run no server, we cannot look you up or identify you in Google's advertising data. The app does not show you an installation ID we could use to find your data. We say this honestly so you know what to expect:

  • Delete all local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
  • Reset or delete your Android advertising ID in Android Settings, Privacy (Ads); open Google's ad privacy form from Settings where it is offered; manage ad personalisation at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Switch off usage & diagnostics sharing in your Android device settings if you do not want Google to collect crash data (section 4.3).
  • Delete your Google backup in your Google account.
  • Write to tembrisstudio@gmail.com with any question or request. We will answer, and we will explain if we cannot identify data as relating to you (GDPR Art. 11).

Under the GDPR you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and object, and lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Under KVKK Art. 11 you may apply to us as data controller (see the Turkish version for the procedure) and complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK).

12. Security

Data sent to AdMob and Google Play travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Local data is kept in the app's private storage.

13. Changes

If we change this policy materially, we will update the effective date above and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent again. Version of 2026-11-05: Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics were removed from the app; earlier versions of the app (before this change) may have sent usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase if you had allowed it.
